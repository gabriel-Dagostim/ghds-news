Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

GHDS News runs only on its owner's computer. It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Data we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and reads the owner's own basic profile (display name, username, avatar), account statistics (followers, following, likes, video count) and the list of their own videos with view, like, comment and share counts. With the video upload permission, the app sends the owner's own video files to their TikTok drafts.

How data is used

Only to show the owner a dashboard of their own accounts and to send their own videos to TikTok. No data is used for advertising or profiling.

Storage

Tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. Tokens can be removed at any time with "Disconnect" in the app or by revoking access in TikTok settings, which deletes them from the app.

Other users

The app does not collect data about other TikTok users.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to read those channels' public statistics. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Cached YouTube data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings, after which the app deletes the stored token.

Contact

Questions: contact the owner through the TikTok account connected to this app.
